Soundboard Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026 (public library and trimmed clips)

Soundboard is an app for Root communities that lets members upload short sounds and play them for everyone in the app. This policy explains what information Soundboard handles and why. Soundboard runs on the Root platform, so Root's Privacy Policy also applies to your use of Root itself.

What we store

  • Sounds you add: only the part you choose to keep (up to 10 seconds), compressed as a small MP3, plus the name, emoji and volume you give it. When you add a sound, your full file is trimmed on your own device and never uploaded. Sounds imported from Discord are stored using Root's file storage.
  • Credits: for sounds added from the public library, the title, author and license of the original sound.
  • Who added each sound: your Root user ID is saved with each sound you upload.
  • Play counts: a total count of how many times each sound has been played, used for the "Top Sounds" list. It is not linked to who played it.

This information is stored per community and is visible to members of the community where the app is installed.

What we share in real time

When you play a sound, the other members who have Soundboard open hear it and see a short notice with your Root nickname (for example, "Alex played Knock Knock"). This notice is not stored.

Public sound library

When you search, the words you type are sent from the Soundboard server to Openverse (run by WordPress.org) to find openly licensed sounds, and sounds you preview or add are downloaded from their original site (for example Freesound). Your name, Root account and other details are not sent with these requests. Openverse's own privacy policy applies to those searches.

What stays on your device

Your volume level, whether you want to hear sounds others play, and your recently used sounds are saved only in your own app storage on your device. They are never sent to us.

What we don't do

  • We don't collect your email address, real name, location, contacts or payment details.
  • We don't use analytics, advertising or tracking tools.
  • We don't sell or share your information with third parties. Data is processed by Root as the platform that hosts the app; library search words go to Openverse as described above.
  • Soundboard does not access your microphone or record voice chat.

Importing from Discord

The optional Discord import tool runs on your own computer. It downloads your Discord server's soundboard sounds to a folder you choose. Nothing is sent to Soundboard until you upload those files yourself.

Keeping and deleting data

Sounds stay in the community until someone deletes them in the app (✎ → Delete), which removes the sound's details from the app. If the app is removed from a community, its data for that community is no longer used. To ask for removal of data about you, contact us using the details below.

Children

Soundboard follows Root's age requirements and does not knowingly collect information from children below the age Root allows.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
